# Art style (reference: style_guide.png)

Prompt prefix for every concept image:

> Stylized low-poly 3D sci-fi RTS asset, chunky readable silhouette, slightly exaggerated proportions,
> hand-painted textures with soft gradients, bevelled edges, minimal fine detail. Off-white and warm
> light-grey armor plates, dark gunmetal joints, small glowing cyan energy lights. Soft studio light
> from top-left. Single object, centered, front three-quarter view, plain light neutral background.

Palette: off-white, light grey, gunmetal, cyan glow, teal crystal, sand-orange terrain, blue-violet shadow.
Team colors (blue/red/green/yellow) are applied in-engine as an accent stripe / ring, not baked into models.

Assets: worker drone, infantry, ranger, hover tank, command HQ, barracks, defense turret,
crystal cluster, mossy boulder. Terrain is a tiled texture, not a model.
